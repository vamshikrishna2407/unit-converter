How to Use Advanced Unit Converter
Features:
  • Multiple Unit Categories: Weight, Length, Temperature, Volume, Area, Time, Speed, Pressure
  • Precision Control: Choose from 2-8 decimal places
  • Scientific Notation: Option for very large/small numbers
  • Copy to Clipboard: Click the copy button next to any result
  • Conversion History: Automatically saves your recent conversions
  • Favorites: Save frequently used conversions
  • Dark/Light Theme: Toggle between themes
Supported Units:
Weight: lbs, grams, kilograms, ounces
Length: meters, miles, inches, feet, centimeters, kilometers
Temperature: celsius, fahrenheit, kelvin
Volume: liters, gallons, milliliters, cups
Area: square meters, square feet, acres
Time: seconds, minutes, hours, days
Speed: mph, km/h, m/s, knots
Pressure: psi, bar, atm, pascal
